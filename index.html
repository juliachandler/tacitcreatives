From docket PDF to Docket Builder — 5 steps

Most county docket viewers won't export to xlsx directly. Here's the one-time setup. Five minutes per case.

  1. Get your docket from the county.
    Sign in to your county prothonotary's online docket viewer (or PA UJS Portal). Find your case. Open the docket sheet — usually downloadable as a PDF or viewable on a web page.
  2. Select all the docket text.
    Open the PDF (or the docket web page). Click in the docket area and press Cmd+A (Mac) or Ctrl+A (Windows). Then copy: Cmd+C / Ctrl+C.
  3. Open a spreadsheet and paste.
    Open Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets. Paste into cell A1. The columns should land roughly as Date / Category / Description, but you may need to tidy them up.
  4. Make sure the first row has these exact headers:
    Date  |  Category  |  Description

    Date in MM/DD/YYYY format. Category is the short type (e.g., ORDER, MOTION, PETITION). Description is the full narrative line from the docket.

  5. Save as .xlsx and upload via Records.
    In Excel: File → Save As → Excel Workbook (.xlsx). In Numbers: File → Export To → Excel. Then come back here, go to Records → Add Record, choose category Dockets, and upload your file. Return to this tab — the Docket Builder auto-parses.
Tip: If your county's docket has multi-line entries that paste badly, just clean up the spreadsheet manually before saving. The five minutes you spend here saves the linking work the app does for you afterward.

📌 What to Expect

Custody cases often last longer than anticipated — sometimes years. During that time, life changes:

  • You may move homes or change jobs
  • Attorneys and judges may change
  • Jurisdictions can shift
  • Family circumstances evolve — illness, loss, new relationships

This is why documentation matters.

The records you keep today may be needed years from now. Export your data regularly and keep backups in a safe place.
