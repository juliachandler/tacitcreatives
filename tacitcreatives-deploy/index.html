What we do

Three ways we help.

For twenty years small businesses had two options: custom software that cost a fortune, or renting the same standardized tools as everyone else and bending to fit them. Those tools were built for the average business, and no business is average. AI has changed the math. Software can now be built around the way you already work, at a price a small business can pay.

Custom Business Applications

Client portals, scheduling systems, intake tools, and job tracking. Built to match how your business runs, with nothing extra. Yours to own, not rent.

Dashboards & Reporting

Turn spreadsheets and manual reports into reliable, easy-to-read information. Answers in minutes instead of weekends.

Workflow Improvement

We look at the repetitive work eating your time and design a simpler way to handle it, using practical AI where it genuinely helps and skipping it where it doesn't.

Good to know

Answers to the questions everyone asks.

Who owns the software?

You do. When the project is done, the code and the data belong to your business, and you can take both anywhere. A fitted tool often replaces several subscriptions you rent today.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays yours and is never sold or shared. We build privacy-first by habit. Even this page loads nothing from third parties.

What happens after launch?

Julia stays available for support, fixes, and improvements. You keep talking to the person who built your system, not a help desk.

What does it cost?

Every project gets a written proposal with a clear cost and timeline before you commit to anything. The first conversation is always free.
